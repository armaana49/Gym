Fat-loss focus · 10:00–11:15

Arms, belly & face

  • You can't burn fat from one spot. Arms, belly and face slim down as your overall body fat drops.
  • The arm and shoulder work tones the muscle underneath, so arms look firmer as fat comes off.
  • Food matters more than the gym: protein and veg every meal, fewer sugary drinks, snacks and takeaways.
  • Plenty of water and less salty food reduces face puffiness quickly.
  • 8,000–10,000 steps a day. Walking to the station counts.
  • Sleep 7–9 hours. Poor sleep makes cravings worse.
  • Weigh or measure your waist once a week, same day and time.
